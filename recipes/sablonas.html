Perfect Chocolate Chip Cookies

★★★★★ (4.8 from 150 reviews)
Prep: 15 mins
Cook: 10 mins
Total: 25 mins
Yields: 24 cookies

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Unsalted Butter, softened
  • 1 cup Brown Sugar, packed
  • 1/2 cup White Sugar
  • 2 large Eggs
  • 3 cups All-Purpose Flour
  • 1 tsp Baking Soda
  • 2 cups Semi-Sweet Chocolate Chips

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Line a tray with parchment paper.
  2. Cream the butter and sugars together until light and fluffy.
  3. Beat in the eggs one at a time, followed by the vanilla extract.
  4. Mix in the dry ingredients slowly until just combined.
  5. Fold in the chocolate chips by hand.
  6. Bake for 10-12 minutes or until edges are golden.

Nutrition (per cookie): 180 kcal | 22g Carbs | 2g Protein | 9g Fat
